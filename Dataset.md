# Dataset

## IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance

This project uses the **IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance** dataset to analyze employee attrition patterns and identify employee characteristics and workplace factors associated with attrition.

The dataset contains employee-level information covering demographics, job characteristics, compensation, satisfaction, work conditions, career progression, and attrition status.

## Dataset Details

- **Employees:** 1,470
- **Variables:** 35
- **Target variable:** Attrition
- **Main areas covered:** Job role, department, monthly income, overtime, job satisfaction, environment satisfaction, work-life balance, job involvement, performance rating, promotions, tenure, stock options, and other employee characteristics.

The analysis uses employee-level records to calculate attrition rates, compare employee groups, examine relationships between workplace factors and attrition, and construct descriptive risk-factor analyses.

## Data Preparation

The dataset was analyzed using **Oracle SQL**. The analysis included calculated measures and derived classifications for:

- Department-level attrition rates
- Overtime and work-life-balance groups
- Relative pay quartiles within job roles
- Promotion gap ratios and stagnation terciles
- Employee risk-factor flags
- Cumulative risk-factor counts
- High-risk employee group comparisons

The analysis is descriptive and observational. The results identify patterns and associations in the dataset but do not establish that individual factors cause employee attrition.
