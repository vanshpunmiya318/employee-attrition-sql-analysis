# Q6: Cumulative Attrition Risk

## Business Question

How does employee attrition change as multiple risk factors accumulate?

## Results

| Risk Factors | Total Employees | Employees Left | Attrition Rate |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 216 | 10 | 4.63% |
| 1 | 560 | 70 | 12.50% |
| 2 | 468 | 83 | 17.74% |
| 3 | 184 | 50 | 27.17% |
| 4 | 36 | 20 | 55.56% |
| 5 | 6 | 4 | 66.67% |

## Key Finding

Attrition increases substantially as the number of identified risk factors increases. Employees with no identified risk factors had an attrition rate of 4.63%, compared with 27.17% among employees with three risk factors and 55.56% among those with four risk factors.

The five-risk group had the highest attrition rate at 66.67%, but contained only 6 employees, so this result should be interpreted cautiously because of the very small sample size.

Overall, the results show a clear association between accumulation of risk factors and higher employee attrition.
